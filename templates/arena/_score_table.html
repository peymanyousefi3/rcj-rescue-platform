Score entry help

Enter raw scores below. Normalized values calculate automatically within each round. Enter the time remaining out of 8:00 as m:ss or whole seconds (0–480); elapsed time is calculated and stored automatically. For example, 2:30 remaining becomes 5:30 elapsed (330 seconds). Leave unknown times blank. Public results show only the sum of recorded elapsed seconds across all rounds, including discarded scores. Use each cell’s upload icon to add or manage its recording. Leave unscored entries blank. Copy a rectangular block of numbers from Excel, click its first destination score, and paste. Rows follow the visible teams; columns follow the rounds. Blank copied cells clear scores. Invalid or oversized blocks are rejected in full. Undo restores the last cell edit or whole paste; Control/⌘ + Z also works. Saving starts a new Undo baseline. Missing scores use a dashed outline and —; zero counts as a score. Tab moves through scores and times; Enter moves down teams. Control/⌘ + Enter saves everything.

Calculation method, discarded rounds and tie-break policy: Competition settings → Challenges & scoring. Each round’s settings contain its all-zero decision. Manual normalized overrides are available for Sum, Average and Best scores; the field-only method always calculates its own ratios.
